## 3. What each checker caught that the others missed

- **🙈 Blind tester, math (g4-math):** wrong choices in q7 with **remainders bigger than the divisor** (true but
  unfinished; a test-wise student crosses them out), w11 not forcing the round-up reading, and an "estimate" that reused
  the exact numbers (twice). The bots passed all of it; the solver agreed with every key.
- **🔎 Fact checker, history (g11-history):** a wrong choice that is **true of a real Black Code** (South Carolina's
  1865 apprenticeship law); and in round 4, told to sweep the whole pack, the **"last federal troops" myth** in three
  more places (A q13's key, B q23's model answer, a review box). The blind rechecks had caught it only in the one item
  they were shown, and round 1's fact checker had kept "Troops left" as a shortening.
- **🔎 Fact checker, science (g8-science):** **three false figures** (a cup, a flask and a bag drawn with the reaction
  already happening in the "before" panel, arrows pointing at the key). The blind tester couldn't see them: the
  student view shows only a figure's labels. It also caught round 3's suggested "Cl₃" trap, which doesn't balance.
- **🙈 No-passage probe (g7-ela):** three fresh testers answered **20 of 20 questions without the passages**, at
  confidence 4–5, on a quiz **Wilco's bots passed**. The bots read choices; they can't see that only the key sounds like
  a teacher's answer, that the stem names the deciding detail, or that Part B retells Part A. This is the order's main
  catch and is now a round 1 gate.
- **🤖 Wilco:** the maker's own runs caught converge at 53% (g4-math) and oddOne 55% (g7-ela) before the counted run;
  counted fails: middle-length 36% (g8-science r2), converge 50%, learnedCV 50% and middle 38% (g7-ela r2–r3). Every
  fail was fixed by changing wrong choices.
- **The lead's preview look** caught what no checker reads: two answer blanks on 24 prompts (g4-math), a rubric
  printing "4 pts" a row (g7-ela), literal `**bold**` (g8-science), and the teacher's capital-letter titles not followed
  (g11-history).

## 4. What failed and why

1. **g7-ela: the questions didn't need the passage, and five rounds couldn't fix it** (9 of 10 a version still right
   without the passage after round 5; 17 FIX open). The passage was written first and the questions fitted to it, so
   most cues were in the passage's plainness, not the choices. Choice-only fixes peeled one layer of cues per round and
   set off the bots in turn (converge → learnedCV → middle). Fix: passage-first design and a round 1 gate (owner
   decisions 1–3, now in `maker.md` v3 and `blind-ela.md` v2).
2. **g11-history: a checker's suggested wording became a false key.** Round 2's recheck suggested "Hayes pulling the
   last federal troops out of the South"; the maker pasted it; only round 3 caught it, at the limit. And there was no
   fact recheck until round 4. Fix: suggestions are directions with their facts flagged; a fact recheck every round on
   changed text and suggested wording; a whole-pack sweep (`facts.md` v2, all `blind-*.md` v2, runbook step 9).
3. **g8-science: an L3 item that kept reusing practised equations** (three rounds), and a maker "pass" read from
   `--items`, which always exits 0 (an extra session). Fix: `maker.md` rules 3 and 6.
4. **The 40% L3 rule was unclear for quizzes:** testers raised it as FIX in g4, g7 and g8; one lead downgraded it. Now:
   tests 30–50%, quizzes at least 1 in 4 a version (`maker.md` and every `blind-*.md`).
5. **Process slips:** `--only` split titles on commas (g11; fixed in the script, the briefs now say `|`); rechecks
   wrote "confirmed" lists instead of `fixed` (g8: the reporter missed 6); parallel checkers each told to write
   `pack.json` (g11); reporter rows with different names for one role and text rounds (g8, g11), which made empty
   duplicate rows in the report; a runner that silently denied web search (g11 round 4).

## 8. Format and renderer gaps (owner, not briefs)

- **No passage field:** passages went in as empty sections, and `checks/student-view.mjs` doesn't print them, so the
  blind tester's view had none (g7-ela). The briefs work around it with a separate passages file; a `passage` field
  printed by the renderer and the student view is the biggest ELA gap.
- `checks.blind[].passageFree` isn't shown in the audit report; no study-guide "know" block (g11); no fill-in type
  (g8); headings left alone at a page foot (g7, g8); `space` is one value for A and B (g8).
- The order page shows `readingquiz` but has no spec fields or source warning for it (`site/feasibility.js` SPECS).
