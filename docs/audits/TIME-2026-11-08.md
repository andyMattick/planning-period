What this says:
1. **The maker's first draft is a third of the whole time** (9.9 of 31 min) and 37% of the cost. Most of its output
   was versions B and C: the same 16 questions with new numbers, each table, r, line and key worked by hand.
2. **The coordinator's gaps are as big as all the checking** (13.1 min against 3.7 min of checker wall clock). They are
   a person reading output, copying folders, writing hand-offs and typing commands: work a script does in seconds.
3. **The checkers are already short and already parallel** (2.2 and 1.5 min). Version A alone would roughly halve the
   blind tester's rows (16 instead of 48); the solver still works every number in every version.
4. Steps that didn't have to wait: the style notes (they don't depend on her answer), the reporter (most of the log is
   numbers a script already has), the print-size check and the browser check (scripts).
