## In one paragraph

Unit 11, the 🔍 Reading Library, is the first of five Georgia-standards English units with no Khan Academy source. The owner asked for it to cover what Units 1–10 don't: reading strategies, main idea and summary, inference and evidence, text structures and features, and reading in context. A 🧭 unit lead mapped it into 5 lessons (one skill each) and ran a writer and a builder at the same time on different files, then merged both and started fresh checkers: a blind solver with the test-wise bot, and six fresh-eyes reviewers. In 1 h 12 min of clock time (20:27 to 21:39 UTC) it produced 70 practice and 40 test-only multiple-choice items, 10 newer-type items, 5 short answers, an essay and 13 term cards. The blind solver agreed with the key on 120 of 120 items and still reported 8 FIX and 4 JUDGMENT; fresh eyes made 3 screen fixes and reported 4 content items; one short round fixed all of it, and a blind recheck agreed on 15 of 15 changed items. It cost $14.61 at API prices as the sessions reported it (27.97 million tokens). The unit lead's and the coordinating session's own work isn't measured.

## What went wrong and was redone

- **Two prompts gave the key away, and three keys were the odd one out or the longest.** The writer ran the fairness gate and the bot first and both passed; the blind solver found them by reading as a student would.
- **The bot has no "least specific" strategy.** The blind solver found that it matters for main-idea items (the key was always the broadest choice), which the bot cannot play. Added to the owner's list below.
- **Three things the builder shipped only the real screens showed:** a Khan sentence on a no-Khan unit, a doubled period in every subject's "The answer is …", and a grown-up label on a kid tile. The builder could not open the unit page locally.
- **One more writer round.** The 8 FIX and the judgment calls cost $1.95 (the first writer session was $4.26); fresh eyes cost $3.43, the most of any checker.
- **The building keeps 🔍, not 📖.** The owner wrote 📖, but 📖 is the Story Corner Library's (Unit 1) icon, and two matching tiles in one town would confuse her. The unit lead decided this at the merge.
