## In one paragraph

Unit 12, the 🎭 Storybook Library, is the second of five Georgia-standards English units with no Khan Academy source. It covers reading literature: plot, conflict and setting; characters and point of view; theme and evidence; figurative language, imagery, mood and tone; and poems, genres and periods. It is the first unit with long passages: stories and poems of 150–260 words shown in a card above each question. A 🧭 unit lead mapped it into 5 lessons, pushed a skeleton with the passage model, and ran a writer and a cheaper-tier builder at the same time on different files, then merged both and started fresh checkers: a blind solver with the test-wise bot, and six fresh-eyes reviewers. In 1 h 50 min of clock time (15:09 to 16:59 UTC) it produced 16 passages, 70 practice and 40 test-only multiple-choice items, 10 newer-type items, 5 short answers, an essay and 15 term cards. The blind solver agreed with the key on 120 of 120 items and still reported 7 FIX and 5 JUDGMENT; fresh eyes fixed the poem layout on phones and found the choices sat too far below the passage; one round of two short sessions fixed all of it, and a cheaper recheck agreed on every changed item and played the unit test. It cost $13.29 at API prices as the sessions reported it (27.59 million tokens). The unit lead's own work isn't measured.

## What went wrong and was redone

- **A quoted public-domain poem had one word changed** ("nurse" for Stevenson's "nursie"). Only the blind solver, checking the text against the 1885 source, caught it; no script checks quotations.
- **Practice and test overlapped in three places**: a line-break example, a theme item and a POV rule practiced in one item and tested in another. The fairness scripts don't compare practice with test items; the blind solver did.
- **The new passage card made the choices hard to find on a phone** (2.5–3 screens down). The builder had checked the card only in a standalone page; fresh eyes measured it in the game, and round 2 added a jump button and a remembered hidden story.
- **Fresh eyes didn't reach the unit test** (its click script stuck on a select-all item), so the cheaper recheck played those screens; it went to about 70 tool calls against a budget of 30, mostly fixing click scripts.
- **One round of two short sessions.** Writer round 2 cost $1.45 (the first writer was $4.73), builder round 2 $0.24; fresh eyes cost $2.48 and the recheck with screens $1.56.
