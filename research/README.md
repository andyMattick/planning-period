# Research collection

Every report, log and audit from building Pet World and the teacher studio (this repo), gathered in one place to share later (owner, Nov 6, 2026).
These are **copies** (snapshots); the originals stay in each repo. Re-run the gather step to refresh them.

## Before anything here is shared publicly

1. **No child data.** Remove the student's name and personal details. Files marked ⚠️ mention her by name.
2. **No secrets** (keys, emails, internal links) and no student work, answers, PINs or saves.
3. **Keep the failures in.** Honest reports of what went wrong are the most useful part.
4. Pick a license for the reports (for example CC BY 4.0) and for any released code (MIT or Apache 2.0).
