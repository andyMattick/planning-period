How it works

Agents taught by teachers, checked by agents that didn't write the work.

  1. Drop in your training documents: your own past worksheets and quizzes, so your agents learn your style.
  2. Drop in your source material: notes, a chapter, the standards, slides.
  3. Pick from the menu.
  4. Add any specific requests.
  5. See the token cost and press Send.
  6. Next, our onboarding agent reads your material and your order and sends you a short follow-up if anything needs a decision (for example: your notes cover 3 lessons, but you asked for 5 days). We start building when you answer, or when you tell us to use our best judgment.
  7. You get a preview (stamped PREVIEW) and the audit report → look it over → request changes or agree → then we release your final, editable files.
  • 📝 MakerWrites the pack from your source, in your style.
  • 🙈 Blind testersOne per subject. They solve the student copy with no key.
  • 🧮 SolverWorks every math answer on its own.
  • 🔎 Fact checkerChecks every claim, date, name and term (science, social studies).
  • 🤖 WilcoTest-wise bots: can a student guess the answer without knowing it?
  • 🧾 ReporterLogs every step and writes your audit report.
  • 🔎 AuditorLearns from each order and your comments, and keeps it affordable.

Build a learning world

Beyond paper packs, the same agent team can build a whole online learning world from your standards: a town where each unit is a building, with practice at three levels and step-by-step hints, note sheets, picked videos, quizzes, a unit test with a review first, a game for every unit and a dashboard for the grown-up.

Worlds are quoted, not priced on the page. The team builds one unit at a time, and you approve each unit before your student sees it.
