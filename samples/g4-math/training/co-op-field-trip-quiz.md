# Co-op Quiz: Dividing (Week 8)

Name: ____________________   Date: ___________

**Your job:** Read each story twice. Show your thinking. Circle the letter or write the answer with its unit.

1) 72 library books go on 8 shelves, the same number on each. How many books on each shelf?
   Answer: ________ books

2) 9 friends share 54 strawberries equally. Which equation shows how many each friend gets?
   (A) 54 + 9 = s   (B) 54 − 9 = s   (C) 9 × 54 = s   (D) 54 ÷ 9 = s

3) Mom bakes 85 muffins and puts 4 in each box. How many boxes can she fill all the way?
   Answer: ________ boxes

4) What is 263 ÷ 6?
   (A) 43 R5   (B) 44 R1   (C) 42 R11   (D) 43 R3

5) ★ Two-Step: The co-op bought 5 bags of 30 balloons. They tie 4 balloons on each table. How many tables get
   balloons?
   Think: What do I find first? ______________________
   Answer: ________ tables

---
KEY (Mr. L):
1) 9 books — 72 ÷ 8 = 9
2) (D) — sharing equally is dividing
3) 21 boxes — 85 ÷ 4 = 21 R1; the 1 left over can't fill a box
4) (A) — 6 × 43 = 258, 263 − 258 = 5
5) 37 tables — 5 × 30 = 150; 150 ÷ 4 = 37 R2; only 37 tables get all 4 balloons
