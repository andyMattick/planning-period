KEY:
1) 54 eggs — 6 × 9
2) $42 — 14 × 3
3) 375 jars — 3 × 125
4) (B) — 389 is close to 400; 6 × 400 = 2,400
5) 162 tomatoes — 4 × 36 = 144, then 144 + 18 = 162
