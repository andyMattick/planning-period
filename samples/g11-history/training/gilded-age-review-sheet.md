# REVIEW SHEET — Unit 6: The Gilded Age

**Directions: Use this sheet to study. The test covers everything in the "Know It Cold" boxes. Practice questions at the end are in the same style as the test.**

## KNOW IT COLD: People
- **Andrew Carnegie** — steel; vertical integration; "The Gospel of Wealth" (1889).
- **John D. Rockefeller** — Standard Oil; horizontal integration; trust.
- **Jane Addams** — Hull House, Chicago (1889); settlement houses.

## KNOW IT COLD: Terms and dates
- **Interstate Commerce Act (1887)** — first federal agency to regulate an industry (railroads).
- **Sherman Antitrust Act (1890)** — outlawed trusts "in restraint of trade"; weakly enforced at first.
- **Political machine** — e.g., Tammany Hall; traded jobs and favors for votes.

## BIG QUESTIONS (be ready to write about one)
