# Density Check-In · Version A
Science 8 · Mr. Okafor

**Directions: Read each question all the way through. Circle the letter of the best answer. For the last part, write in complete sentences.**

### Part 1 · Know it
1) Density tells us
   (A) how much space an object takes up
   (B) how much mass is packed into a certain volume
   (C) how heavy an object feels in your hand
   (D) how fast an object sinks

2) A block has a mass of 24 g and a volume of 8 cm³. What is its density?
   (A) 3 g/cm³   (B) 16 g/cm³   (C) 32 g/cm³   (D) 192 g/cm³

### Part 2 · Use it
3) A rubber duck floats in water. Which statement must be true?
   (A) The duck has less mass than the water in the tub.
   (B) The duck is less dense than water.
   (C) The duck has a smaller volume than the water.
   (D) The duck is filled with air.

### Part 3 · Explain it
4) Kayla says a big log must be denser than a small pebble because the log is heavier. Is she right? Use the words
   **mass** and **volume** in your answer.
   ______________________________________________
   ______________________________________________

---
Teacher key (my style): 1) B  2) A (24 ÷ 8 = 3)  3) B  4) No. Density is mass divided by volume; the log has more
mass but also much more volume, so its density can be lower. Look for: mass AND volume named, a "because".
