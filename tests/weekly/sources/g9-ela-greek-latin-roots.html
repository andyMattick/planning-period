Unit 3 Vocabulary: Greek and Latin Roots

Many English words are built from Greek and Latin roots. If you know the root, you can often work out a word you have never seen.

RootMeaningExamples

Using roots as clues

Break the word into parts: a prefix, a root, a suffix. Malnutrition = mal (bad) + nutrition (feeding): poor feeding. Then check the meaning against the sentence it is in.

Context still matters: port in portable means carry, but a port on the coast is a different word.
